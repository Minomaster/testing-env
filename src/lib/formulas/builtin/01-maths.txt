@category Maths

= Quadratic formula
latex: x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
note: Roots of a x² + b x + c = 0. Reference only; solve a specific quadratic in the calculator.
tags: algebra, roots, polynomial

= Pythagorean theorem
latex: c^2 = a^2 + b^2
expr: c_h^2 = a^2 + b^2
v: c_h | 1 | hypotenuse
v: a | 1 | first leg
v: b | 1 | second leg
tags: geometry, triangle, right angle

= Law of cosines
latex: c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos\gamma
expr: c_s^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2 a b cos(γ)
v: c_s | 1 | side opposite γ
v: a | 1 | side a
v: b | 1 | side b
v: γ | rad | angle between a and b
tags: geometry, triangle, trigonometry

= Law of sines
latex: \frac{a}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{b}{\sin\beta}
expr: a / sin(α_a) = b / sin(β_b)
v: a | 1 | side opposite α
v: α_a | rad | angle opposite a
v: b | 1 | side opposite β
v: β_b | rad | angle opposite b
tags: geometry, triangle, trigonometry

= Pythagorean identity
latex: \sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1
tags: trigonometry, identity

= Double-angle identities
latex: \sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta,\quad \cos 2\theta = \cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta
tags: trigonometry, identity

= Angle-sum identities
latex: \sin(a \pm b) = \sin a\cos b \pm \cos a\sin b,\quad \cos(a \pm b) = \cos a\cos b \mp \sin a\sin b
tags: trigonometry, identity

= Euler's formula
latex: e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta
note: Basis of phasor notation: A∠θ = A e^{iθ}.
tags: complex, phasor, exponential

= Logarithm rules
latex: \ln(ab) = \ln a + \ln b,\quad \ln\frac{a}{b} = \ln a - \ln b,\quad \ln a^n = n\ln a
tags: logarithm, algebra

= Change of logarithm base
latex: \log_b x = \frac{\ln x}{\ln b}
expr: y = ln(x) / ln(b)
v: y | 1 | log base b of x
v: x | 1 | argument
v: b | 1 | base
tags: logarithm

= Small-angle approximations
latex: \sin\theta \approx \tan\theta \approx \theta,\quad \cos\theta \approx 1 - \frac{\theta^2}{2}
note: θ in radians; good to ~1% below about 14°.
tags: approximation, trigonometry

= Binomial approximation
latex: (1 + x)^n \approx 1 + nx \quad (|x| \ll 1)
tags: approximation, series

= Taylor series
latex: f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x - a)^n
tags: series, calculus
