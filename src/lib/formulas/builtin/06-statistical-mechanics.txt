@category Statistical mechanics

= Boltzmann factor (population ratio)
latex: \frac{N_2}{N_1} = e^{-(E_2 - E_1)/k_BT}
expr: N_ratio = exp(-ΔE / (k_B T))
v: N_ratio | 1 | population ratio N₂/N₁
v: ΔE | eV | energy difference E₂ − E₁
v: T | K | temperature
tags: statistical mechanics, boltzmann

= Boltzmann entropy
latex: S = k_B \ln \Omega
expr: S = k_B ln(Ω)
v: S | J/K | entropy
v: Ω | 1 | number of microstates
tags: statistical mechanics, entropy

= Partition function
latex: Z = \sum_i g_i\, e^{-E_i/k_BT},\quad P_i = \frac{g_i e^{-E_i/k_BT}}{Z}
tags: statistical mechanics, partition function

= Helmholtz free energy
latex: F = U - TS = -k_BT\ln Z
expr: F = U - T S
v: F | J | Helmholtz free energy
v: U | J | internal energy
v: T | K | temperature
v: S | J/K | entropy
tags: statistical mechanics, thermodynamics, free energy

= Gibbs free energy
latex: G = H - TS
expr: G_f = H - T S
v: G_f | J | Gibbs free energy
v: H | J | enthalpy
v: T | K | temperature
v: S | J/K | entropy
tags: thermodynamics, free energy, chemistry

= Equipartition theorem
latex: \langle E\rangle = \tfrac{f}{2}k_BT
expr: E = f k_B T / 2
v: E | J | mean energy per particle
v: f | 1 | degrees of freedom
v: T | K | temperature
tags: statistical mechanics, kinetic theory

= Maxwell–Boltzmann most probable speed
latex: v_p = \sqrt{\frac{2k_BT}{m}}
expr: v_p = sqrt(2 k_B T / m)
v: v_p | m/s | most probable speed
v: T | K | temperature
v: m | kg | particle mass
tags: statistical mechanics, kinetic theory, gas

= Maxwell–Boltzmann mean speed
latex: \langle v\rangle = \sqrt{\frac{8k_BT}{\pi m}}
expr: v_mean = sqrt(8 k_B T / (π m))
v: v_mean | m/s | mean speed
v: T | K | temperature
v: m | kg | particle mass
tags: statistical mechanics, kinetic theory, gas

= Fermi–Dirac distribution
latex: f(E) = \frac{1}{e^{(E - \mu)/k_BT} + 1}
expr: f = 1 / (exp((E - μ) / (k_B T)) + 1)
v: f | 1 | occupation probability
v: E | eV | state energy
v: μ | eV | chemical potential (Fermi level)
v: T | K | temperature
tags: statistical mechanics, fermions, semiconductors

= Bose–Einstein distribution
latex: n(E) = \frac{1}{e^{(E - \mu)/k_BT} - 1}
expr: n = 1 / (exp((E - μ) / (k_B T)) - 1)
v: n | 1 | mean occupation number
v: E | eV | state energy
v: μ | eV | chemical potential
v: T | K | temperature
tags: statistical mechanics, bosons

= Planck's law (spectral radiance)
latex: B_\lambda(T) = \frac{2hc^2}{\lambda^5}\frac{1}{e^{hc/\lambda k_BT} - 1}
expr: B = 2 h_P c^2 / λ^5 / (exp(h_P c / (λ k_B T)) - 1)
v: B | W/m^3 | spectral radiance (per steradian, per metre of wavelength)
v: λ | m | wavelength
v: T | K | temperature
tags: radiation, black body, quantum
