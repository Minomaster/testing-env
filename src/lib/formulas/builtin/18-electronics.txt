@category Electronics

= Inverting amplifier gain
latex: A_v = -\frac{R_f}{R_\text{in}}
expr: A_v = -R_f / R_in
v: A_v | 1 | voltage gain
v: R_f | Ω | feedback resistor
v: R_in | Ω | input resistor
tags: op-amp, amplifier, gain

= Non-inverting amplifier gain
latex: A_v = 1 + \frac{R_f}{R_g}
expr: A_v = 1 + R_f / R_g
v: A_v | 1 | voltage gain
v: R_f | Ω | feedback resistor
v: R_g | Ω | resistor to ground
tags: op-amp, amplifier, gain

= Summing amplifier (two inputs)
latex: V_\text{out} = -R_f\left(\frac{V_1}{R_1} + \frac{V_2}{R_2}\right)
expr: V_out = -R_f (V_1 / R_1 + V_2 / R_2)
v: V_out | V | output voltage
v: R_f | Ω | feedback resistor
v: V_1 | V | input 1
v: R_1 | Ω | input resistor 1
v: V_2 | V | input 2
v: R_2 | Ω | input resistor 2
tags: op-amp, amplifier, summing

= Difference amplifier
latex: V_\text{out} = \frac{R_f}{R_1}(V_2 - V_1)
expr: V_out = R_f (V_2 - V_1) / R_1
v: V_out | V | output voltage
v: R_f | Ω | feedback resistor (= R to ground on + input)
v: V_2 | V | non-inverting input
v: V_1 | V | inverting input
v: R_1 | Ω | input resistors
tags: op-amp, amplifier, differential

= Op-amp integrator
latex: V_\text{out}(t) = -\frac{1}{RC}\int_0^t V_\text{in}\,dt
tags: op-amp, integrator

= Op-amp differentiator
latex: V_\text{out} = -RC\frac{dV_\text{in}}{dt}
tags: op-amp, differentiator

= Gain–bandwidth product
latex: f_{-3\,\mathrm{dB}} = \frac{\mathrm{GBW}}{A_v}
expr: f_c = GBW / A_v
v: f_c | Hz | closed-loop bandwidth
v: GBW | Hz | gain–bandwidth product
v: A_v | 1 | closed-loop gain
tags: op-amp, bandwidth

= Slew-rate-limited frequency

v: f_max | Hz | full-power bandwidth
v: SR | V/s | slew rate

tags: op-amp, slew rate, bandwidth

= MOSFET saturation current
latex: I_D = \tfrac{1}{2}k_n(V_{GS} - V_{th})^2
expr: I_D = k_n (V_GS - V_th)^2 / 2
v: I_D | A | drain current
v: k_n | A/V^2 | process transconductance × W/L
v: V_GS | V | gate–source voltage
v: V_th | V | threshold voltage
tags: mosfet, transistor

= MOSFET triode current
latex: I_D = k_n\left[(V_{GS} - V_{th})V_{DS} - \tfrac{1}{2}V_{DS}^2\right]
expr: I_D = k_n ((V_GS - V_th) V_DS - V_DS^2 / 2)
v: I_D | A | drain current
v: k_n | A/V^2 | process transconductance × W/L
v: V_GS | V | gate–source voltage
v: V_th | V | threshold voltage
v: V_DS | V | drain–source voltage (< V_GS − V_th)
tags: mosfet, transistor

= MOSFET transconductance
latex: g_m = \frac{2I_D}{V_{GS} - V_{th}}
expr: g_m = 2 I_D / (V_GS - V_th)
v: g_m | S | transconductance
v: I_D | A | drain current
v: V_GS | V | gate–source voltage
v: V_th | V | threshold voltage
tags: mosfet, transistor, small signal

= LED series resistor

v: V_s | V | supply voltage

v: I_f | A | LED current
tags: led, resistor, diode

= Zener regulator series resistor
latex: R = \frac{V_\text{in} - V_Z}{I_Z + I_L}
expr: R = (V_in - V_Z) / (I_Z + I_L)
v: R | Ω | series resistor
v: V_in | V | input voltage
v: V_Z | V | zener voltage
v: I_Z | A | zener current
v: I_L | A | load current
tags: zener, regulator, diode

= Full-wave rectifier average voltage

v: V_avg | V | average output voltage
v: V_p | V | peak voltage
tags: rectifier, diode, power supply
