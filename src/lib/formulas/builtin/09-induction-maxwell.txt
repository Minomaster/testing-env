@category Induction & Maxwell's equations

= Faraday's law (average EMF)
latex: \mathcal{E} = -N\frac{\Delta\Phi_B}{\Delta t}
expr: EMF = N ΔΦ / Δt
v: EMF | V | induced EMF magnitude
v: N | 1 | number of turns
v: ΔΦ | Wb | change in flux
v: Δt | s | time taken
tags: induction, faraday, emf

= Motional EMF
latex: \mathcal{E} = BLv
expr: EMF = B L v
v: EMF | V | induced EMF
v: B | T | magnetic flux density
v: L | m | rod length
v: v | m/s | speed perpendicular to B
tags: induction, emf

= Peak EMF of a rotating coil (generator)
latex: \mathcal{E}_0 = NBA\omega
expr: EMF = N B A ω
v: EMF | V | peak EMF
v: N | 1 | number of turns
v: B | T | magnetic flux density
v: A | m^2 | coil area
v: ω | rad/s | angular velocity
tags: induction, generator, emf, ac

= Inductance definition
latex: L = \frac{N\Phi_B}{I}
expr: L = N Φ / I
v: L | H | inductance
v: N | 1 | number of turns
v: Φ | Wb | flux through one turn
v: I | A | current
tags: inductor, inductance

= Solenoid inductance
latex: L = \frac{\mu_0\mu_r N^2 A}{l}
expr: L = µ_0 µ_r N^2 A / l
v: L | H | inductance
v: µ_r | 1 | relative permeability of the core
v: N | 1 | number of turns
v: A | m^2 | cross-sectional area
v: l | m | length
tags: inductor, inductance, solenoid

= Voltage across an inductor
latex: V = L\frac{dI}{dt}

v: V | V | voltage (average over Δt)
v: L | H | inductance
v: ΔI | A | change in current
v: Δt | s | time taken
tags: inductor, induction

= Energy stored in an inductor

v: U | J | stored energy

= Ideal transformer (voltages)
latex: \frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p}
expr: V_s / V_p = N_s / N_p
v: V_s | V | secondary voltage
v: V_p | V | primary voltage
v: N_s | 1 | secondary turns
v: N_p | 1 | primary turns
tags: transformer, ac, power

= Ideal transformer (currents)
latex: \frac{I_s}{I_p} = \frac{N_p}{N_s}
expr: I_s / I_p = N_p / N_s
v: I_s | A | secondary current
v: I_p | A | primary current
v: N_p | 1 | primary turns
v: N_s | 1 | secondary turns
tags: transformer, ac, power

= Displacement current

expr: I_d = ε_0 ΔΦ_E / Δt
v: I_d | A | displacement current
v: ΔΦ_E | V m | change in electric flux
v: Δt | s | time taken
tags: maxwell, displacement current

= Gauss's law (differential form)
latex: \nabla\cdot\vec E = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}
tags: maxwell, gauss, electrostatics

= Gauss's law for magnetism
latex: \nabla\cdot\vec B = 0
tags: maxwell, magnetism

= Faraday's law (differential form)
latex: \nabla\times\vec E = -\frac{\partial\vec B}{\partial t}
tags: maxwell, faraday, induction

= Ampère–Maxwell law (differential form)
latex: \nabla\times\vec B = \mu_0\vec J + \mu_0\varepsilon_0\frac{\partial\vec E}{\partial t}
tags: maxwell, ampere, magnetism

= Maxwell's equations (integral form)
latex: \oint\vec E\cdot d\vec A = \frac{Q}{\varepsilon_0},\ \oint\vec B\cdot d\vec A = 0,\ \oint\vec E\cdot d\vec l = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt},\ \oint\vec B\cdot d\vec l = \mu_0 I + \mu_0\varepsilon_0\frac{d\Phi_E}{dt}
tags: maxwell, electromagnetism
