@category Special relativity

= Lorentz factor
latex: \gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}}
expr: γ = 1 / sqrt(1 - v^2 / c^2)
v: γ | 1 | Lorentz factor
v: v | m/s | speed
tags: relativity, lorentz

= Time dilation
latex: \Delta t = \gamma\,\Delta t_0
expr: Δt = Δt_0 / sqrt(1 - v^2 / c^2)
v: Δt | s | time in the frame where the clock moves
v: Δt_0 | s | proper time
v: v | m/s | relative speed
tags: relativity, time dilation

= Length contraction
latex: L = \frac{L_0}{\gamma}
expr: L = L_0 sqrt(1 - v^2 / c^2)
v: L | m | contracted length
v: L_0 | m | proper length
v: v | m/s | relative speed
tags: relativity, length contraction

= Relativistic momentum
latex: p = \gamma m v
expr: p = m v / sqrt(1 - v^2 / c^2)
v: p | kg m/s | momentum
v: m | kg | rest mass
v: v | m/s | speed
tags: relativity, momentum

= Total relativistic energy
latex: E = \gamma m c^2
expr: E = m c^2 / sqrt(1 - v^2 / c^2)
v: E | J | total energy
v: m | kg | rest mass
v: v | m/s | speed
tags: relativity, energy

= Mass–energy equivalence
latex: E_0 = mc^2
expr: E = m c^2
v: E | J | rest energy
v: m | kg | rest mass
tags: relativity, energy, nuclear

= Relativistic kinetic energy
latex: K = (\gamma - 1)mc^2
expr: K = (1 / sqrt(1 - v^2 / c^2) - 1) m c^2
v: K | J | kinetic energy
v: v | m/s | speed
v: m | kg | rest mass
tags: relativity, energy

= Energy–momentum relation
latex: E^2 = (pc)^2 + (mc^2)^2
expr: E^2 = (p c)^2 + (m c^2)^2
v: E | J | total energy
v: p | kg m/s | momentum
v: m | kg | rest mass
tags: relativity, energy, momentum

= Relativistic velocity addition
latex: u = \frac{v + u'}{1 + vu'/c^2}
expr: u = (v + u_p) / (1 + v u_p / c^2)
v: u | m/s | velocity in the ground frame
v: v | m/s | frame velocity
v: u_p | m/s | velocity in the moving frame
tags: relativity, velocity

= Relativistic Doppler effect
latex: f_o = f_s\sqrt{\frac{1 + \beta}{1 - \beta}},\quad \beta = \frac{v}{c}
expr: f_o = f_s sqrt((1 + v / c) / (1 - v / c))
v: f_o | Hz | observed frequency
v: f_s | Hz | source frequency
v: v | m/s | approach speed (negative when receding)
tags: relativity, doppler, light
