= Thermal voltage
latex: V_T = \frac{k_B T}{q}
expr: V_th = k_B T / q_e
v: V_th | V | thermal voltage (≈ 25.85 mV at 300 K)
v: T | K | temperature
tags: semiconductors, diode, thermal voltage

= Shockley diode equation
latex: I = I_S\left(e^{V_D/(nV_T)} - 1\right)
expr: I = I_S (exp(q_e V_D / (n k_B T)) - 1)
v: I | A | diode current
v: I_S | A | saturation current
v: V_D | V | diode voltage
v: n | 1 | ideality factor (1–2)
v: T | K | temperature
tags: semiconductors, diode, pn junction

v: n | m^-3 | electron concentration
v: p | m^-3 | hole concentration
v: n_i | m^-3 | intrinsic carrier concentration
tags: semiconductors, carriers, doping

= Intrinsic carrier concentration
latex: n_i = \sqrt{N_c N_v}\;e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}
expr: n_i = sqrt(N_c N_v) exp(-E_g / (2 k_B T))
v: n_i | m^-3 | intrinsic carrier concentration
v: N_c | m^-3 | effective density of states, conduction band
v: N_v | m^-3 | effective density of states, valence band
v: E_g | eV | band gap
v: T | K | temperature
tags: semiconductors, carriers, band gap

= Carrier concentration from the Fermi level
latex: n = n_i\,e^{(E_F - E_i)/k_BT}
expr: n = n_i exp(ΔE_F / (k_B T))
v: n | m^-3 | electron concentration
v: n_i | m^-3 | intrinsic carrier concentration
v: ΔE_F | eV | Fermi level above the intrinsic level
v: T | K | temperature
tags: semiconductors, fermi level, doping

= Conductivity of a semiconductor

v: n | m^-3 | electron concentration
v: μ_n | m^2/(V s) | electron mobility
v: p | m^-3 | hole concentration
v: μ_p | m^2/(V s) | hole mobility
tags: semiconductors, conductivity, mobility

= Built-in potential of a pn junction
latex: V_{bi} = \frac{k_BT}{q}\ln\frac{N_A N_D}{n_i^2}
expr: V_bi = k_B T ln(N_A N_D / n_i^2) / q_e
v: V_bi | V | built-in potential
v: T | K | temperature
v: N_A | m^-3 | acceptor concentration
v: N_D | m^-3 | donor concentration
v: n_i | m^-3 | intrinsic carrier concentration
tags: semiconductors, pn junction, diode

= Depletion width
latex: W = \sqrt{\frac{2\varepsilon_s (V_{bi} - V)}{q}\left(\frac{1}{N_A} + \frac{1}{N_D}\right)}
expr: W = sqrt(2 ε_0 ε_r V_j (1 / N_A + 1 / N_D) / q_e)
v: W | m | depletion width
v: ε_r | 1 | relative permittivity (11.7 for Si)
v: V_j | V | junction voltage (V_bi − V applied)
v: N_A | m^-3 | acceptor concentration
v: N_D | m^-3 | donor concentration
tags: semiconductors, pn junction, depletion

= Junction capacitance
latex: C_j = \frac{\varepsilon_s A}{W}
expr: C_j = ε_0 ε_r A / W
v: C_j | F | depletion capacitance
v: ε_r | 1 | relative permittivity
v: A | m^2 | junction area
v: W | m | depletion width
tags: semiconductors, pn junction, capacitance

= Resistance temperature dependence

v: α | K^-1 | temperature coefficient
v: ΔT | K | temperature change
tags: materials, resistance, temperature, rtd
