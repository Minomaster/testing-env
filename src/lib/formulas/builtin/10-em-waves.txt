@category EM waves & transmission lines

= Speed of light from ε₀ and μ₀
latex: c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}}
note: Evaluates to 299 792 458 m/s; the calculator has it as c.
tags: em waves, light, maxwell

= Phase velocity in a medium
latex: v = \frac{c}{n} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu\varepsilon}}
expr: v = c / sqrt(ε_r µ_r)
v: v | m/s | phase velocity
v: ε_r | 1 | relative permittivity
v: µ_r | 1 | relative permeability
tags: em waves, dielectric, velocity

= Refractive index from permittivity
latex: n = \sqrt{\varepsilon_r\mu_r}
expr: n = sqrt(ε_r µ_r)
v: n | 1 | refractive index
v: ε_r | 1 | relative permittivity
v: µ_r | 1 | relative permeability
tags: em waves, optics, dielectric

= E and B amplitudes in a plane wave
latex: E = cB
expr: E = c B
v: E | V/m | electric field amplitude
v: B | T | magnetic field amplitude
tags: em waves, plane wave

= Intensity of a plane wave
latex: I = \tfrac{1}{2}c\varepsilon_0 E_0^2
expr: I = c ε_0 E_0^2 / 2
v: I | W/m^2 | time-averaged intensity
v: E_0 | V/m | electric field amplitude
tags: em waves, intensity, energy

= Radiation pressure
latex: p = \frac{I}{c}\ \text{(absorbed)},\quad p = \frac{2I}{c}\ \text{(reflected)}
expr: p = I / c
v: p | Pa | radiation pressure on a perfect absorber
v: I | W/m^2 | intensity
tags: em waves, pressure, light

= Photon energy flux to photon rate

tags: em waves, photons, light

= Skin depth
latex: \delta = \sqrt{\frac{2\rho}{\omega\mu}}

tags: em waves, conductor, skin effect, ac

= Characteristic impedance of a lossless line
latex: Z_0 = \sqrt{\frac{L'}{C'}}
expr: Z_c = sqrt(L_l / C_l)
v: Z_c | Ω | characteristic impedance
v: L_l | H/m | inductance per metre
v: C_l | F/m | capacitance per metre
tags: transmission line, impedance

= Phase velocity on a lossless line
latex: v_p = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L'C'}}
expr: v_p = 1 / sqrt(L_l C_l)
v: v_p | m/s | phase velocity
v: L_l | H/m | inductance per metre
v: C_l | F/m | capacitance per metre
tags: transmission line, velocity

= Coaxial cable impedance
latex: Z_0 = \frac{\eta_0}{2\pi\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}}\ln\frac{D}{d} \approx \frac{60\,\Omega}{\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}}\ln\frac{D}{d}
expr: Z_c = Z_0 ln(D / d) / (2 π sqrt(ε_r))
v: Z_c | Ω | characteristic impedance
v: ε_r | 1 | dielectric relative permittivity
v: D | m | inner diameter of the outer conductor
v: d | m | diameter of the inner conductor
note: Z_0 here is the impedance of free space (≈ 376.73 Ω).
tags: transmission line, coax, impedance

= Reflection coefficient
latex: \Gamma = \frac{Z_L - Z_0}{Z_L + Z_0}
expr: Γ = (Z_L - Z_c) / (Z_L + Z_c)
v: Γ | 1 | reflection coefficient (resistive load)
v: Z_L | Ω | load impedance
v: Z_c | Ω | line characteristic impedance
note: For complex loads, compute Γ in the calculator with complex impedances.
tags: transmission line, reflection, matching

= Voltage standing wave ratio
latex: \mathrm{VSWR} = \frac{1 + |\Gamma|}{1 - |\Gamma|}
expr: VSWR = (1 + Γ) / (1 - Γ)
v: VSWR | 1 | voltage standing wave ratio
v: Γ | 1 | magnitude of the reflection coefficient
tags: transmission line, reflection, matching

= Return loss
latex: RL = -20\log_{10}|\Gamma|
expr: RL = -20 log(Γ)
v: RL | 1 | return loss in dB
v: Γ | 1 | magnitude of the reflection coefficient
tags: transmission line, reflection, decibel

= Free-space path loss
latex: \mathrm{FSPL} = \left(\frac{4\pi d f}{c}\right)^2

v: FSPL | 1 | free-space path loss in dB
v: d | m | distance
v: f | Hz | frequency
tags: antenna, propagation, rf, decibel

= Friis transmission equation
latex: \frac{P_r}{P_t} = G_t G_r\left(\frac{\lambda}{4\pi d}\right)^2
expr: P_r = P_t G_t G_r (λ / (4 π d))^2
v: P_r | W | received power
v: P_t | W | transmitted power
v: G_t | 1 | transmit antenna gain (linear)
v: G_r | 1 | receive antenna gain (linear)
v: λ | m | wavelength
v: d | m | distance
tags: antenna, propagation, rf

= Half-wave dipole length
latex: L \approx \frac{\lambda}{2} = \frac{c}{2f}
expr: L = c / (2 f)
v: L | m | dipole length (ignoring end effects, ≈ 5% shorter in practice)
v: f | Hz | frequency
tags: antenna, rf
