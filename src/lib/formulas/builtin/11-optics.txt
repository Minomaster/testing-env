@category Optics

= Refractive index
latex: n = \frac{c}{v}
expr: n = c / v
v: n | 1 | refractive index
v: v | m/s | speed of light in the medium
tags: optics, refraction

= Snell's law
latex: n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2
expr: n_1 sin(θ_1) = n_2 sin(θ_2)
v: n_1 | 1 | refractive index of medium 1
v: θ_1 | rad | angle of incidence
v: n_2 | 1 | refractive index of medium 2
v: θ_2 | rad | angle of refraction
tags: optics, refraction

= Critical angle
latex: \sin\theta_c = \frac{n_2}{n_1}
expr: sin(θ_c) = n_2 / n_1
v: θ_c | rad | critical angle
v: n_2 | 1 | refractive index of the outer (lower) medium
v: n_1 | 1 | refractive index of the inner (higher) medium
tags: optics, total internal reflection, fibre

= Numerical aperture of a fibre
latex: \mathrm{NA} = \sqrt{n_\text{core}^2 - n_\text{clad}^2}
expr: NA = sqrt(n_core^2 - n_clad^2)
v: NA | 1 | numerical aperture
v: n_clad | 1 | cladding index
v: n_core | 1 | core index
tags: optics, fibre

= Brewster's angle
latex: \tan\theta_B = \frac{n_2}{n_1}
expr: tan(θ_B) = n_2 / n_1
v: θ_B | rad | Brewster angle
v: n_2 | 1 | refractive index of the second medium
v: n_1 | 1 | refractive index of the first medium
tags: optics, polarization

= Malus's law
latex: I = I_0\cos^2\theta
expr: I = I_0 cos(θ)^2
v: I | W/m^2 | transmitted intensity
v: I_0 | W/m^2 | incident (polarized) intensity
v: θ | rad | angle between polarization and polarizer axis
tags: optics, polarization

= Thin lens equation
latex: \frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{d_o} + \frac{1}{d_i}
expr: 1 / f = 1 / d_o + 1 / d_i
v: f | m | focal length
v: d_o | m | object distance
v: d_i | m | image distance
tags: optics, lens

= Magnification
latex: m = -\frac{d_i}{d_o} = \frac{h_i}{h_o}
expr: M = d_i / d_o
v: M | 1 | magnification (magnitude)
v: d_i | m | image distance
v: d_o | m | object distance
tags: optics, lens, mirror

= Lensmaker's equation
latex: \frac{1}{f} = (n - 1)\left(\frac{1}{R_1} - \frac{1}{R_2}\right)
expr: 1 / f = (n - 1) (1 / R_1 - 1 / R_2)
v: f | m | focal length
v: n | 1 | lens refractive index
v: R_1 | m | radius of the first surface
v: R_2 | m | radius of the second surface
tags: optics, lens

= Spherical mirror focal length
latex: f = \frac{R}{2}
expr: f = R_c / 2
v: f | m | focal length
v: R_c | m | radius of curvature
tags: optics, mirror

= Optical power
latex: P = \frac{1}{f}
expr: P = 1 / f
v: P | m^-1 | optical power in dioptres
v: f | m | focal length
tags: optics, lens

= Double-slit bright fringes
latex: y_m = \frac{m\lambda L}{d}
expr: y = m λ L / d
v: y | m | fringe position from the centre
v: m | 1 | order
v: λ | m | wavelength
v: L | m | distance to the screen
v: d | m | slit separation
tags: optics, interference, young

= Double-slit interference condition
latex: d\sin\theta = m\lambda
expr: d sin(θ) = m λ
v: d | m | slit separation
v: θ | rad | angle to the bright fringe
v: m | 1 | order
v: λ | m | wavelength
tags: optics, interference

= Single-slit minima
latex: a\sin\theta = m\lambda
expr: a sin(θ) = m λ
v: a | m | slit width
v: θ | rad | angle to the dark fringe
v: m | 1 | order (1, 2, …)
v: λ | m | wavelength
tags: optics, diffraction

= Diffraction grating
latex: d\sin\theta = m\lambda,\quad d = \frac{1}{N}
expr: sin(θ) / N = m λ
v: θ | rad | angle of the maximum
v: N | m^-1 | lines per metre
v: m | 1 | order
v: λ | m | wavelength
tags: optics, diffraction, spectroscopy

= Rayleigh criterion
latex: \theta \approx 1.22\frac{\lambda}{D}
expr: θ = 1.22 λ / D
v: θ | rad | minimum resolvable angle
v: λ | m | wavelength
v: D | m | aperture diameter
tags: optics, diffraction, resolution

= Thin-film constructive reflection
latex: 2nt = \left(m + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\lambda
expr: 2 n t = (m + 0.5) λ
v: n | 1 | film refractive index
v: t | m | film thickness
v: m | 1 | order (0, 1, 2, …)
v: λ | m | wavelength in vacuum
note: For one phase-flipping reflection (e.g. soap film in air).
tags: optics, interference, thin film

= Bragg's law
latex: 2d\sin\theta = n\lambda
expr: 2 d sin(θ) = n λ
v: d | m | lattice plane spacing
v: θ | rad | glancing angle
v: n | 1 | order
v: λ | m | wavelength
tags: optics, diffraction, x-ray, crystal

= Apparent depth
latex: d' = d\,\frac{n_2}{n_1}
expr: d_a = d n_2 / n_1
v: d_a | m | apparent depth
v: d | m | real depth
v: n_2 | 1 | index of the observer's medium
v: n_1 | 1 | index of the object's medium
tags: optics, refraction
