@category Power & machines

= DC motor back EMF
latex: E = k\omega
expr: EMF = k_m ω
v: EMF | V | back EMF
v: k_m | V s | motor constant
v: ω | rad/s | shaft speed
tags: motor, dc machine

= DC motor torque
latex: \tau = kI
expr: τ = k_m I
v: τ | N m | torque
v: k_m | N m/A | motor (torque) constant
v: I | A | armature current
tags: motor, dc machine, torque

= DC motor voltage equation
latex: V = IR_a + k\omega
expr: V = I R_a + k_m ω
v: V | V | terminal voltage
v: I | A | armature current
v: R_a | Ω | armature resistance
v: k_m | V s | motor constant
v: ω | rad/s | shaft speed
tags: motor, dc machine

= Mechanical power
latex: P = \tau\omega
expr: P = τ ω
v: P | W | mechanical power
v: τ | N m | torque
v: ω | rad/s | angular speed
tags: motor, power, rotation

= Synchronous speed
latex: \omega_s = \frac{4\pi f}{p}
expr: ω_s = 4 π f / p
v: ω_s | rad/s | synchronous speed
v: f | Hz | supply frequency
v: p | 1 | number of poles
note: In rpm: n_s = 120 f / p.
tags: motor, induction machine, synchronous

= Induction motor slip
latex: s = \frac{n_s - n}{n_s}
expr: s = (n_s - n) / n_s
v: s | 1 | slip
v: n_s | rad/s | synchronous speed
v: n | rad/s | rotor speed
tags: motor, induction machine

= Transmission line loss
latex: P_\text{loss} = I^2 R = \left(\frac{P}{V}\right)^2 R
expr: P_loss = (P / V)^2 R
v: P_loss | W | resistive loss
v: P | W | delivered power
v: V | V | transmission voltage
v: R | Ω | line resistance
tags: power, transmission, loss

= Energy cost
latex: \text{cost} = P\,t\cdot\text{price}
expr: cost = P t price
v: cost | 1 | cost (currency)
v: P | W | power
v: t | s | time
v: price | J^-1 | price per joule (1 kWh = 3.6e6 J)
tags: power, energy
