= Bernoulli's equation
latex: p_1 + \tfrac{1}{2}\rho v_1^2 + \rho g h_1 = p_2 + \tfrac{1}{2}\rho v_2^2 + \rho g h_2
expr: p_1 + ρ v_1^2 / 2 + ρ g h_1 = p_2 + ρ v_2^2 / 2 + ρ g h_2
v: p_1 | Pa | pressure at point 1
v: ρ | kg/m^3 | density
v: v_1 | m/s | speed at point 1
v: g | m/s^2 | gravitational acceleration
v: h_1 | m | height of point 1
v: p_2 | Pa | pressure at point 2
v: v_2 | m/s | speed at point 2
v: h_2 | m | height of point 2
tags: fluids, flow, energy

= Poiseuille's law
latex: Q = \frac{\pi r^4 \Delta p}{8 \eta L}

v: r | m | pipe radius
v: Δp | Pa | pressure difference
v: η | Pa s | dynamic viscosity
v: L | m | pipe length
tags: fluids, viscosity, flow

v: v | m/s | flow speed
v: L | m | characteristic length
v: η | Pa s | dynamic viscosity
tags: fluids, turbulence, flow
