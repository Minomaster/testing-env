@category Nuclear physics

= Radioactive decay
latex: N = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}
expr: N = N_0 exp(-λ t)
v: N | 1 | remaining nuclei
v: N_0 | 1 | initial nuclei
v: λ | s^-1 | decay constant
v: t | s | time
tags: nuclear, decay, radioactivity

= Decay with half-life
latex: N = N_0\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^{t/t_{1/2}}
expr: N = N_0 0.5^(t / t_half)
v: N | 1 | remaining nuclei (or activity, mass)
v: N_0 | 1 | initial amount
v: t | s | time
v: t_half | s | half-life
tags: nuclear, decay, half-life

= Half-life and decay constant
latex: t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{\lambda}
expr: t_half = ln(2) / λ
v: t_half | s | half-life
v: λ | s^-1 | decay constant
tags: nuclear, decay, half-life

= Mean lifetime
latex: \tau = \frac{1}{\lambda}
expr: τ = 1 / λ
v: τ | s | mean lifetime
v: λ | s^-1 | decay constant
tags: nuclear, decay

= Activity
latex: A = \lambda N
expr: A = λ N
v: A | Hz | activity in becquerel (decays per second)
v: λ | s^-1 | decay constant
v: N | 1 | number of nuclei
tags: nuclear, decay, activity

= Binding energy from mass defect
latex: E_B = \Delta m\,c^2
expr: E_B = Δm c^2
v: E_B | eV | binding energy
v: Δm | kg | mass defect
note: 1 u of mass defect ≈ 931.494 MeV. In the calculator: 1 u * c^2 to MeV.
tags: nuclear, binding energy, relativity

= Nuclear radius
latex: R = R_0 A^{1/3},\quad R_0 \approx 1.2\ \mathrm{fm}
expr: r = 1.2e-15 m A^(1/3)
v: r | m | nuclear radius
v: A | 1 | mass number
tags: nuclear, nucleus

= Absorbed dose
latex: D = \frac{E}{m}
expr: D = E / m
v: D | J/kg | absorbed dose in gray
v: E | J | absorbed energy
v: m | kg | mass
tags: nuclear, radiation, dosimetry

= Equivalent dose
latex: H = w_R D
expr: H = w_R D
v: H | J/kg | equivalent dose in sievert
v: w_R | 1 | radiation weighting factor (1 for γ/β, 20 for α)
v: D | J/kg | absorbed dose in gray
tags: nuclear, radiation, dosimetry

= Attenuation of radiation
latex: I = I_0 e^{-\mu x}
expr: I = I_0 exp(-µ x)
v: I | W/m^2 | transmitted intensity
v: I_0 | W/m^2 | incident intensity
v: µ | m^-1 | linear attenuation coefficient
v: x | m | thickness
tags: nuclear, radiation, shielding
