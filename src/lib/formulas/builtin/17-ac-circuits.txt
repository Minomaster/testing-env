@category AC circuits

= Capacitive reactance
latex: X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C} = \frac{1}{2\pi f C}
expr: X_C = 1 / (2 π f C)
v: X_C | Ω | capacitive reactance
v: f | Hz | frequency
v: C | F | capacitance
tags: ac, reactance, capacitor, impedance

= Inductive reactance
latex: X_L = \omega L = 2\pi f L
expr: X_L = 2 π f L
v: X_L | Ω | inductive reactance
v: f | Hz | frequency
v: L | H | inductance
tags: ac, reactance, inductor, impedance

= Complex impedances
latex: Z_R = R,\quad Z_L = j\omega L,\quad Z_C = \frac{1}{j\omega C}
note: In the calculator (i is the imaginary unit): Z_L = 2 pi f L i, Z_C = 1 / (2 pi f C i).
tags: ac, impedance, phasor, complex

= Series RLC impedance magnitude
latex: |Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2}
expr: Z = sqrt(R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2)
v: Z | Ω | impedance magnitude
v: R | Ω | resistance
v: X_L | Ω | inductive reactance
v: X_C | Ω | capacitive reactance
tags: ac, impedance, rlc

= Series RLC phase angle
latex: \varphi = \arctan\frac{X_L - X_C}{R}
expr: tan(φ) = (X_L - X_C) / R
v: φ | rad | phase of voltage relative to current
v: X_L | Ω | inductive reactance
v: X_C | Ω | capacitive reactance
v: R | Ω | resistance
tags: ac, phase, rlc, phasor

= Resonant frequency
latex: f_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}
expr: f_0 = 1 / (2 π sqrt(L C))
v: f_0 | Hz | resonant frequency
v: L | H | inductance
v: C | F | capacitance
tags: ac, resonance, rlc, lc

= Series RLC quality factor
latex: Q = \frac{1}{R}\sqrt{\frac{L}{C}}
expr: Q = sqrt(L / C) / R
v: Q | 1 | quality factor
v: L | H | inductance
v: C | F | capacitance
v: R | Ω | resistance
tags: ac, resonance, rlc, q factor

= Bandwidth and Q
latex: BW = \frac{f_0}{Q}
expr: BW = f_0 / Q
v: BW | Hz | −3 dB bandwidth
v: f_0 | Hz | centre (resonant) frequency
v: Q | 1 | quality factor
tags: ac, resonance, bandwidth, filter

= RC low-pass / high-pass cutoff
latex: f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}
expr: f_c = 1 / (2 π R C)
v: f_c | Hz | −3 dB cutoff frequency
v: R | Ω | resistance
v: C | F | capacitance
tags: ac, filter, rc, cutoff

= RL cutoff frequency
latex: f_c = \frac{R}{2\pi L}
expr: f_c = R / (2 π L)
v: f_c | Hz | −3 dB cutoff frequency
v: R | Ω | resistance
v: L | H | inductance
tags: ac, filter, rl, cutoff

= First-order low-pass magnitude
latex: |H(f)| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + (f/f_c)^2}}
expr: H = 1 / sqrt(1 + (f / f_c)^2)
v: H | 1 | gain magnitude (linear)
v: f | Hz | frequency
v: f_c | Hz | cutoff frequency
tags: ac, filter, frequency response

= RMS of a sine wave
latex: V_\text{rms} = \frac{V_p}{\sqrt{2}}
expr: V_rms = V_p / sqrt(2)
v: V_rms | V | RMS voltage
v: V_p | V | peak voltage
tags: ac, rms, sine

= Peak-to-peak voltage
latex: V_{pp} = 2V_p
expr: V_pp = 2 V_p
v: V_pp | V | peak-to-peak voltage
v: V_p | V | peak (amplitude) voltage
tags: ac, sine

= Average (real) power
latex: P = V_\text{rms} I_\text{rms}\cos\varphi
expr: P = V_rms I_rms cos(φ)
v: P | W | real power
v: V_rms | V | RMS voltage
v: I_rms | A | RMS current
v: φ | rad | phase angle between voltage and current
tags: ac, power, power factor

= Apparent power
latex: S = V_\text{rms} I_\text{rms}
expr: S = V_rms I_rms
v: S | W | apparent power (VA)
v: V_rms | V | RMS voltage
v: I_rms | A | RMS current
tags: ac, power

= Reactive power
latex: Q = V_\text{rms} I_\text{rms}\sin\varphi
expr: Q_r = V_rms I_rms sin(φ)
v: Q_r | W | reactive power (var)
v: V_rms | V | RMS voltage
v: I_rms | A | RMS current
v: φ | rad | phase angle
tags: ac, power

= Power triangle
latex: S^2 = P^2 + Q^2
expr: S^2 = P^2 + Q_r^2
v: S | W | apparent power (VA)
v: P | W | real power
v: Q_r | W | reactive power (var)
tags: ac, power, power factor

= Power factor
latex: \mathrm{pf} = \frac{P}{S} = \cos\varphi
expr: pf = P / S
v: pf | 1 | power factor
v: P | W | real power
v: S | W | apparent power
tags: ac, power, power factor

= Three-phase line and phase voltage (star)
latex: V_L = \sqrt{3}\,V_\text{ph}
expr: V_L = sqrt(3) V_ph
v: V_L | V | line-to-line voltage
v: V_ph | V | phase (line-to-neutral) voltage
tags: ac, three-phase, power

= Three-phase power
latex: P = \sqrt{3}\,V_L I_L\cos\varphi
expr: P = sqrt(3) V_L I_L cos(φ)
v: P | W | total real power
v: V_L | V | line voltage
v: I_L | A | line current
v: φ | rad | phase angle
tags: ac, three-phase, power

= Admittance
latex: Y = \frac{1}{Z} = G + jB
note: In the calculator: Y = 1 / Z with a complex Z.
tags: ac, admittance, impedance, complex
