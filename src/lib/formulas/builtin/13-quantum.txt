= Compton scattering
latex: \Delta\lambda = \frac{h}{m_e c}(1 - \cos\theta)

v: Δλ | m | wavelength shift

tags: quantum, compton, photon

= Heisenberg uncertainty (position–momentum)
latex: \Delta x\,\Delta p \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}
expr: Δx Δp = hbar / 2
v: Δx | m | position uncertainty (minimum case)
v: Δp | kg m/s | momentum uncertainty
note: Solving gives the minimum-uncertainty case.
tags: quantum, uncertainty

= Hydrogen energy levels (Bohr)
latex: E_n = -\frac{m_e e^4}{8\varepsilon_0^2 h^2}\frac{1}{n^2} \approx -\frac{13.6\ \mathrm{eV}}{n^2}
expr: E = -m_e q_e^4 / (8 ε_0^2 h_P^2 n^2)
v: E | eV | energy of level n
v: n | 1 | principal quantum number
tags: quantum, atomic, hydrogen, bohr

= Rydberg formula
latex: \frac{1}{\lambda} = R_\infty\left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)
expr: 1 / λ = R_inf (1 / n_1^2 - 1 / n_2^2)
v: λ | m | emitted wavelength
v: n_1 | 1 | lower level
v: n_2 | 1 | upper level
tags: quantum, atomic, hydrogen, spectroscopy

= Tunnelling probability (thick barrier)
latex: T \approx e^{-2\kappa L},\quad \kappa = \frac{\sqrt{2m(V_0 - E)}}{\hbar}
expr: T = exp(-2 L sqrt(2 m (V_0 - E)) / hbar)
v: T | 1 | transmission probability
v: L | m | barrier width
v: m | kg | particle mass
v: V_0 | eV | barrier height
v: E | eV | particle energy
tags: quantum, tunnelling

= Bohr magneton
latex: \mu_B = \frac{e\hbar}{2m_e}
note: ≈ 9.274 × 10⁻²⁴ J/T. In the calculator: q_e hbar / (2 m_e).
tags: quantum, atomic, magnetism, spin

= Zeeman splitting
