= Magnetic force on a moving charge

expr: F = q v B sin(θ)
v: F | N | magnetic force
v: q | C | charge
v: v | m/s | speed
v: B | T | magnetic flux density
v: θ | rad | angle between v and B
tags: magnetism, force, lorentz

= Lorentz force
latex: \vec F = q(\vec E + \vec v\times\vec B)
note: In the calculator: q (E + cross(v, B)).
